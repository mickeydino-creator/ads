## Composition
Product centred, occupies ~45-60% of frame height, lots of charcoal negative space, horizon line of the studio sweep behind lower third.

## Camera
Reference views: front, 3/4 left/right, profile, back, top-down, low 3/4, macro on cap. Our orbit uses the same family of angles in one continuous move instead of cuts.

## Identity
Silver square label with dark serif "NOIR", brushed cylinder cap, black glossy body. Kept identical in every frame because it is one 3D model.

## Research match
Proportions measured from the collar/front views and encoded in src/bottle.ts constants.

## Anti-pattern
No slideshow of the 9 tiles, no glow, no coloured haze, no chaotic explosion at the impact.
