## Product or concept
BEFORE / AFTER - portfolio spot for a Hebrew motion-design service aimed at Israeli small businesses. A cluttered, typical local flyer for a fictional barbershop ("מספרה 17", invented, no real business) is torn away; the same information is re-set into a premium animated ad. 15 s, 9:16 (1080x1920), Instagram Reels, Hebrew RTL. CTA: send a DM.

## Authoritative sources
User brief only. Business, address, hours and phone (050-000-0000 placeholder) are fictional and marked as such on the end frame. Fonts: Rubik, Secular One, Frank Ruhl Libre (SIL OFL). Original synthesized score.

## Real visual language
Before: yellow flyer, red starburst, five fonts, outline type, exclamation marks, clip-art scissors, dashed border.
After: warm white #f2ede4, ink #141210, one flat accent brick red #c8321e. Frank Ruhl Libre 900 wordmark, Rubik UI. Thin rules, line-drawn scissors, flat diagonal barber-pole stripes. No 3D.

## Closest approved mechanism
Continuous transformation with match cuts (as in FORM / VANTA): clutter falls away in strips, the surviving content travels and restyles into the clean layout, scissors cut words into the next word, the pole stripe band floods the frame on the 9.0 s drop, before/after split, the "after" grows into the end frame.

## Motion plan
120 BPM, beat = 15 frames; events in src/barber/timeline.json.
0-2 s flyer + hook. 2-3 s tape-stop, strips tear away. 3-5 s content restyles into the clean layout. 5-9 s wordmark + pole band, scissors snip service words on beats. 9 s drop: stripes flood. 9.5-11 s "open on Friday" word stack. 11-13 s before/after split, old flyer cut and dropped. 13-15 s CTA end frame, stable hold from 14.2 s.

## Avoid
3D, glow, gradients, glassmorphism, neon, particles, generic templates, real business names or logos, Instagram logo, unreadable Hebrew, wrong RTL order, key text outside the Reels safe zone (y 220-1500).
