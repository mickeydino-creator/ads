## Product or concept
MUSIC IN MOTION - unofficial portfolio concept inspired by the Spotify listening experience. Not commissioned or endorsed by Spotify; no Spotify logo used. End frame carries the disclaimer. 15 s, 9:16 Reels.

## Authoritative sources
User brief. All albums, artists and tracks are fictional originals (Neon Hours / Lumen Vale, Summer Fever / Sol Arcade, Midnight Drive / Nova Lane, Bass Theory / Grid Kid). Original synthesized score.

## Real visual language
Acid green #c6ff1a, black, purple #7a2cff, hot pink #ff2e93, warm white; per-world accents orange/blue. Unbounded 800/900 display, Manrope UI. Must differ from the FORM Studio spot (Anton, orange/black/warm white).

## Closest approved mechanism
Continuous transformation with match cuts: wave -> letters -> EQ bars -> radial EQ -> iris -> album art -> player -> colour blocks -> sun -> moon -> wave lines -> EQ bars -> wave -> ring -> three rings -> album frames -> type roll -> end frame.

## Motion plan
120 BPM (beat = 15 frames); events in src/pulse/timeline.json.

## Avoid
Spotify logo or altered marks, fake official campaign, 3D, generic visualizer templates, glow, random movement.
